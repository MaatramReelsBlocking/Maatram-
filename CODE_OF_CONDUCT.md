# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Maatram is built by school students and used by school students. Some of the people in
this repository are minors. Act like it.

## Expected

- Be civil. Disagree with the code, not the person.
- Assume the other person is new, because they usually are.
- Explain your reasoning when you reject something. "No" without a why is not review.
- Respect that maintainers here have exams and are not on call.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, anywhere in the project or its channels.
- Contacting maintainers privately after being asked not to.
- Publishing anyone's personal information — school, address, phone number, or photographs.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or the Discord.

## Scope

This applies to the repository, the issue tracker, pull requests, the Discord server and
any public space where someone is representing Maatram.

## Reporting

Email **maatram97@gmail.com**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept private.

If a report involves a minor's safety, we will involve a responsible adult. We are not able
to promise confidentiality in that case, and we will say so before acting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break these
rules, and may block accounts. First response is usually a warning; repeat or serious
behaviour is a ban.
